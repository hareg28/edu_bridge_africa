EduBridge Africa is an international education consultancy based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We help students explore international study options, identify suitable academic programmes, understand admission requirements and navigate their next steps.

We believe education can transform lives, strengthen communities and support sustainable development across Africa. Our mission is to make international education more accessible by building meaningful connections between students and educational institutions around the world.
