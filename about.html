TrainHub Africa PLC is a legally registered Ethiopian company connecting students and professionals with quality international education, training, and career opportunities.

OUR PURPOSE

Learn. Grow.
Succeed.

We work with national and international educational institutions, universities, and training providers to help students access suitable academic programs and empower professionals with skills for a global environment.

Our founding team brings international study experience, cross-cultural understanding, and a commitment to educational excellence. We are ICEF-certified and committed to professional standards and ethical practices in international student recruitment.

From free consultations and academic profile assessment to university selection, application and scholarship guidance, visa support, and pre-departure planning, we guide students throughout their journey.

Our mission is to make international education and professional training more accessible, transparent, and achievable for students and professionals across Africa.
